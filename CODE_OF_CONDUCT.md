# Behavectl Community Conduct

Behavectl is infrastructure for changing persistent AI-agent behavior. The
community should be as careful with people as the project is with behavior
changes.

## Expected

- Discuss ideas and implementations, not personal worth.
- Assume good intent, but require evidence for technical claims.
- Be specific about what was actually tested versus simulated.
- Respect privacy: do not post private prompts, credentials, proprietary code,
  or retained agent traces without permission.
- Make room for beginners to ask precise questions without ridicule.
- Disagree directly and professionally.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or sexualized conduct.
- Doxxing or sharing private repository / agent data.
- Knowingly presenting fixture results as real model evidence.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions after a maintainer asks to stop.

## Enforcement

For ordinary project disagreements, use the relevant issue or pull request.
For sensitive conduct or security matters, use private repository reporting
mechanisms once the public repository is available.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict
participation when needed to keep the project safe and productive.
